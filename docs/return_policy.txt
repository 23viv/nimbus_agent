NIMBUS HOME GOODS — RETURN POLICY
Last updated: August 2026

==============================================
OVERVIEW
==============================================
At Nimbus, we want you to love every item you bring into your home. If something isn't right, we make returns simple and hassle-free.

==============================================
RETURN WINDOW
==============================================
You may return most items within 30 days of delivery. The return window begins on the date your order is marked as "Delivered" by the carrier.

For items purchased between November 1 and December 31 (our Holiday Season), the return window is extended to January 31 of the following year.

==============================================
ELIGIBLE ITEMS
==============================================
To be eligible for a return, items must meet ALL of the following conditions:
- Item is in its original, unused condition
- Item is in its original packaging with all tags attached
- Item has not been assembled, installed, or altered
- Item was purchased directly from Nimbus (nimbus.com or the Nimbus mobile app)

==============================================
NON-RETURNABLE ITEMS
==============================================
The following items cannot be returned under any circumstances:
- Custom or personalized items (monogrammed, engraved, made-to-order)
- Final sale / clearance items (marked clearly at time of purchase)
- Perishable goods (candles burned more than 1 hour, diffuser oils once opened)
- Mattresses and pillows (for hygiene reasons), unless defective
- Gift cards

==============================================
HOW TO INITIATE A RETURN
==============================================
1. Log in to your Nimbus account at nimbus.com
2. Navigate to Orders → select the order → click "Return Item(s)"
3. Select the item(s) and reason for return
4. Print the prepaid return shipping label (emailed to you within 5 minutes)
5. Drop the package at any authorized carrier location within 7 days of generating the label

==============================================
REFUND TIMELINE
==============================================
- Refunds are processed within 5–7 business days of us receiving the returned item
- Refunds are issued to the original payment method only
- Shipping charges are non-refundable unless the return is due to our error (wrong item shipped, defective product)
- You will receive an email confirmation once your refund is processed

==============================================
EXCHANGES
==============================================
Nimbus does not currently offer direct exchanges. To exchange an item:
1. Return the original item following the process above
2. Place a new order for the replacement item
You may place the new order before we receive your return to avoid stock running out.

==============================================
DAMAGED OR DEFECTIVE ITEMS
==============================================
If you receive a damaged or defective item:
- Contact our support team within 7 days of delivery
- Provide your order number and photos of the damage
- We will arrange a replacement or full refund at no cost, including shipping
- You do not need to return damaged items in most cases (our team will advise)

==============================================
THIRD-PARTY MARKETPLACE PURCHASES
==============================================
Items purchased through third-party sellers (Amazon, Walmart Marketplace, etc.) are subject to that marketplace's return policy, not Nimbus's policy. Please contact the marketplace directly.

==============================================
CONTACT SUPPORT
==============================================
For return questions, contact Nimbus Support:
- Chat: nimbus.com/support (available Mon–Fri 9am–6pm ET)
- Email: support@nimbus.com (response within 1 business day)
- Phone: 1-800-NIMBUS-1 (Mon–Fri 9am–6pm ET)
