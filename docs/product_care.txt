NIMBUS HOME GOODS — PRODUCT CARE GUIDE
Last updated: August 2026

==============================================
OVERVIEW
==============================================
Proper care extends the life of your Nimbus products significantly. Below are care instructions organized by product category. Always check the care label attached to your specific item, as individual products may have specific requirements.

==============================================
BEDDING & LINENS
==============================================

Sheets & Pillowcases:
- Machine wash in cold or warm water (max 104°F / 40°C) on a gentle or normal cycle
- Use mild, liquid detergent; avoid bleach unless the product is labeled bleach-safe
- Tumble dry on low heat; remove promptly to minimize wrinkles
- Do not dry clean
- Iron on medium heat if desired; for linen blends, slightly damp ironing is recommended

Duvet Covers & Comforters:
- Machine wash on gentle cycle with warm water and mild detergent
- Use a large-capacity washer (front-loading preferred) to allow proper agitation
- Tumble dry on low with 2–3 clean dryer balls to prevent clumping; may require multiple cycles
- Ensure fully dry before storing to prevent mold/mildew
- Store in a breathable cotton bag, not a plastic bag

Pillows:
- Most Nimbus pillows are machine washable; check individual care label
- Wash 2 pillows at a time to balance the load
- Use warm water and mild detergent; run an extra spin cycle to remove moisture
- Tumble dry on low with dryer balls until completely dry (may take 2–3 cycles)
- Down/feather pillows: add 15 minutes of air-fluff cycle after drying to restore loft

==============================================
TOWELS
==============================================
- Machine wash in warm water (not hot) on a regular cycle
- Avoid fabric softener — it reduces absorbency over time; use white vinegar as a natural alternative
- Tumble dry on medium heat; remove while slightly damp and air-finish to preserve softness
- Wash new towels before first use to remove manufacturing residue and improve absorbency
- Do not bleach colored towels; use color-safe detergent only

==============================================
RUGS
==============================================

Small Rugs (under 3' x 5'):
- Machine wash on gentle cycle in cold water; lay flat or hang to dry (do not tumble dry)
- Vacuum regularly on low suction setting; avoid beater bar for delicate weaves

Large Rugs (3' x 5' and above):
- Spot-clean with cold water and mild soap; blot (do not rub) stains immediately
- Professional cleaning recommended every 12–18 months for wool or hand-knotted rugs
- Rotate 180° every 6 months for even wear
- Use a rug pad to prevent slipping and reduce wear

General Rug Care:
- Vacuum in the direction of the pile; avoid fringe when vacuuming
- For shedding in new rugs: normal for first 4–6 weeks; will diminish with regular vacuuming
- Air out rugs outdoors on a dry day periodically to freshen and remove dust

==============================================
CANDLES
==============================================
- First burn: Allow the wax pool to reach the edge of the jar before extinguishing (typically 2–4 hours); this prevents tunneling
- Trim the wick to ¼ inch before each use to ensure a clean, even burn and minimize soot
- Maximum continuous burn time: 4 hours; allow to cool 2 hours before relighting
- Keep away from drafts, vents, and flammable materials
- Burn on a heat-resistant surface; never leave unattended
- Stop burning when ½ inch of wax remains to protect the container
- To extinguish: use a candle snuffer or gently blow; do not use water
- Store candles in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight to prevent fading and warping

==============================================
DIFFUSERS & ESSENTIAL OILS
==============================================
- Keep diffusers away from direct sunlight and heat sources to preserve scent
- Wipe the interior of ultrasonic diffusers weekly with a cotton swab dipped in white vinegar
- Do not mix different Nimbus reed diffuser oils in the same vessel
- Reed diffusers: flip reeds every 1–2 weeks to refresh scent; fewer reeds = subtler scent, more reeds = stronger scent
- Essential oils are concentrated; keep out of reach of children and pets

==============================================
CERAMIC & STONEWARE
==============================================
- Most Nimbus ceramics are dishwasher safe; check the base of the item for dishwasher-safe symbol
- Hand wash recommended for glazed decorative pieces and items with metallic accents (gold/silver rims)
- Microwave safe unless item has metallic accents
- Avoid sudden temperature changes (e.g., moving directly from freezer to hot oven) to prevent cracking
- Store stacked items with a soft cloth or paper towel between pieces to prevent scratching

==============================================
WOOD & BAMBOO
==============================================
- Hand wash only; never soak or put in dishwasher (causes warping and cracking)
- Dry immediately after washing; do not air dry while wet
- Oil cutting boards and wooden utensils monthly with food-safe mineral oil or beeswax conditioner
- Keep away from prolonged direct sunlight to prevent fading and drying
- For bamboo: wipe with damp cloth; avoid harsh chemicals
