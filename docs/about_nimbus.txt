ABOUT NIMBUS — COMPANY OVERVIEW
Last updated: August 2026

==============================================
WHO WE ARE
==============================================
Nimbus is a premium furniture company dedicated to bringing beautiful, functional, and sustainable furniture into modern homes. Founded with a simple belief — that great design should be accessible to everyone — Nimbus offers a curated collection of sofas, beds, dining tables, chairs, shelving, desks, and home storage solutions crafted for everyday living.

Our products are designed in-house and manufactured by trusted partners who share our commitment to quality craftsmanship, responsible materials, and ethical production.

==============================================
OUR MISSION
==============================================
To design furniture that lasts — in style, in quality, and in purpose. We believe your home should feel like you: comfortable, considered, and uniquely your own.

==============================================
WHAT WE SELL
==============================================
Nimbus offers furniture across every room in your home:

- Living Room: Sofas, sectionals, armchairs, coffee tables, TV units, bookshelves
- Bedroom: Beds, bed frames, headboards, nightstands, wardrobes, dressers
- Dining Room: Dining tables, dining chairs, benches, sideboards, bar carts
- Home Office: Desks, office chairs, filing cabinets, shelving units, monitor stands
- Outdoor: Patio sets, garden chairs, outdoor loungers, weatherproof storage
- Kids & Youth: Bunk beds, study desks, toy storage, children's chairs

==============================================
OUR MATERIALS
==============================================
We source responsibly:
- Solid hardwoods (oak, walnut, beech) and FSC-certified engineered wood
- High-density foam seating with durable fabric and genuine/faux leather upholstery options
- Powder-coated steel and brushed brass metal frames
- Non-toxic, water-based finishes and adhesives throughout

==============================================
SUSTAINABILITY
==============================================
Nimbus is committed to reducing our environmental footprint:
- 80% of our packaging is made from recycled or biodegradable materials
- We partner with reforestation programs to offset wood sourcing
- Our warehouses run on 100% renewable energy
- We offer a Furniture Take-Back Program: when you replace a Nimbus piece, we arrange free collection and responsibly recycle or donate the old item

==============================================
DESIGN PHILOSOPHY
==============================================
Every Nimbus piece is designed by our in-house team with three principles in mind:
1. Form follows function — beautiful furniture that actually works for real life
2. Longevity over trends — timeless aesthetics that won't look dated in five years
3. Modularity where possible — pieces that can grow and adapt with your space

==============================================
FREQUENTLY ASKED ABOUT NIMBUS
==============================================

Q: Is Nimbus a physical or online-only store?
A: Both! You can shop at nimbus.com or in one of our showrooms located in New York, Los Angeles, Chicago, and Austin. Our showrooms are open Mon–Sat 10am–7pm and Sun 11am–5pm.

Q: Where is Nimbus furniture made?
A: Our furniture is designed in New York and manufactured by certified partners in Portugal, Vietnam, and the United States. Every factory is independently audited against our Supplier Code of Conduct.

Q: Can I try furniture before buying online?
A: Yes. Our showrooms carry most of our current collection. You can also use our Nimbus AR App (available on iOS and Android) to preview furniture in your actual room before ordering.

Q: Do you offer interior design services?
A: Nimbus Premium members get free access to the Nimbus Design Concierge — a virtual room planning service where our designers help you choose and arrange furniture for any room. Standard members can purchase this service for $75/session.

Q: How long do Nimbus products last?
A: Our solid wood and upholstered furniture is built to last 10–20 years under normal household use. All products include a 1-year limited warranty, and many structural components carry an extended 5-year warranty.
