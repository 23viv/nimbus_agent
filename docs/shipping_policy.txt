NIMBUS HOME GOODS — SHIPPING POLICY
Last updated: August 2026

==============================================
OVERVIEW
==============================================
Nimbus offers domestic and international shipping options to get your home goods delivered quickly and reliably. All shipping is handled through our trusted carrier network.

==============================================
DOMESTIC SHIPPING (United States)
==============================================

Standard Shipping:
- Delivery time: 5–7 business days
- Cost: $5.99 flat rate; FREE on orders over $75
- Available to all 50 US states, including Alaska and Hawaii (add 2–3 extra days for AK/HI)

Expedited Shipping:
- Delivery time: 2–3 business days
- Cost: $14.99 flat rate
- Not available for oversized/freight items

Overnight Shipping:
- Delivery time: Next business day (order must be placed by 12pm ET)
- Cost: $29.99 flat rate
- Not available for oversized/freight items, Alaska, or Hawaii

Oversized / Freight Items (furniture, large rugs):
- Delivery time: 7–14 business days
- Cost: Calculated at checkout based on item and delivery zip code
- White-glove delivery (room placement + packaging removal) available as an add-on: $49

==============================================
INTERNATIONAL SHIPPING
==============================================
Nimbus ships to the following countries and regions:

Canada:
- Delivery time: 7–12 business days
- Cost: From $12.99 (calculated at checkout by weight/dimensions)
- Duties and taxes: Collected at checkout (DDP — Delivered Duty Paid); no surprise fees on delivery

United Kingdom & European Union:
- Delivery time: 10–15 business days
- Cost: From $19.99 (calculated at checkout)
- Duties and taxes: Collected at checkout (DDP)

Australia & New Zealand:
- Delivery time: 12–18 business days
- Cost: From $24.99 (calculated at checkout)
- Duties and taxes: Not included; recipient is responsible for any customs fees on delivery

Rest of World:
- Currently NOT supported. Nimbus does not ship to countries outside the regions listed above.
- Customers in unsupported regions may use a package forwarding service at their own risk; Nimbus cannot be responsible for forwarding-related issues.

==============================================
PROCESSING TIME
==============================================
- Most in-stock orders are processed and handed to the carrier within 1–2 business days of placing the order
- Orders placed on weekends or US federal holidays begin processing the next business day
- Custom or personalized items require 3–5 additional business days for production before shipping
- You will receive a shipping confirmation email with tracking information once your order has been picked up by the carrier

==============================================
ORDER TRACKING
==============================================
- Track your order at nimbus.com/track or via the link in your shipping confirmation email
- Tracking updates may take 12–24 hours to appear after your shipping confirmation email is sent

==============================================
UNDELIVERABLE PACKAGES
==============================================
If a package is returned to us as undeliverable (wrong address, unclaimed, refused):
- We will contact you via email within 2 business days
- You may request a reship (additional shipping charge applies) or a refund minus original shipping cost
- Refunds for undeliverable packages are processed within 10 business days of the package returning to our warehouse

==============================================
LOST OR STOLEN PACKAGES
==============================================
- If your tracking shows "Delivered" but you haven't received your package, wait 24 hours (carriers occasionally scan packages early)
- After 24 hours, contact Nimbus Support with your order number
- We will open a carrier investigation (typically 5–7 business days)
- If the investigation confirms the package was lost, we will reship or refund at no cost

==============================================

- Email: support@nimbus.com (response within 1 business day)
