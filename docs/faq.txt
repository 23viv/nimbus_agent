NIMBUS HOME GOODS — FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ)
Last updated: August 2026

==============================================
ORDERING
==============================================

Q: Can I modify or cancel my order after placing it?
A: Orders can be modified or cancelled within 1 hour of placement. After that, the order enters our fulfillment system and changes are not possible. To attempt a modification, contact support immediately via live chat at nimbus.com/support — this is the fastest channel for time-sensitive requests. If the order has already shipped, you can return it once received.

Q: Do you offer price matching?
A: Nimbus does not offer price matching with competitors. However, if an item you purchased goes on sale at Nimbus.com within 7 days of your purchase, contact support and we'll apply the sale price as a store credit.

Q: Can I order by phone?
A: No, Nimbus accepts orders exclusively through nimbus.com and the Nimbus mobile app. Our support team cannot place orders on your behalf.

Q: Do you offer gift wrapping?
A: Yes! Gift wrapping is available for most items at $5 per item. Select the gift wrap option at checkout. You can add a personalized message (up to 150 characters) at no extra charge.

Q: What payment methods do you accept?
A: We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and Nimbus Gift Cards. We do not accept checks, money orders, or cash on delivery.

==============================================
ACCOUNT & MEMBERSHIP
==============================================

Q: What is Nimbus Premium?
A: Nimbus Premium is our paid membership plan at $9.99/month or $89.99/year. Benefits include:
  - Free standard shipping on all orders (no minimum)
  - Early access to sales and new product launches
  - Members-only discounts (10% off all orders)
  - Priority customer support
  Free account holders get standard support and pay regular shipping rates.

Q: How do I upgrade from a free account to Nimbus Premium?
A: Log in to nimbus.com → click your name/avatar in the top right → select "Upgrade to Premium" → choose monthly or annual billing → enter payment details. Your premium benefits activate immediately.

Q: How do I reset my password?
A: Click "Forgot Password" on the login page, enter your email address, and follow the link sent to your inbox. Password reset links expire after 30 minutes. If you didn't receive the email, check your spam folder or contact support.

Q: Can I have multiple shipping addresses saved to my account?
A: Yes, you can save up to 10 shipping addresses in your account under Settings → Addresses.

Q: How do I delete my account?
A: Account deletion requests can be submitted via email at privacy@nimbus.com. Please note: deleting your account will permanently remove your order history and any remaining store credit. Orders that are still in transit or within the return window must be resolved before account deletion can proceed.

==============================================
WARRANTY
==============================================

Q: What warranty does Nimbus offer?
A: All Nimbus products carry a 1-year limited warranty against manufacturing defects from the date of purchase. This covers:
  - Structural failures under normal use
  - Stitching or seam failures in textiles
  - Glaze defects in ceramics
  - Electronic failures in diffusers and humidifiers

Q: What is NOT covered by the warranty?
A: The warranty does not cover:
  - Normal wear and tear
  - Damage from improper use, accidents, or failure to follow care instructions
  - Fading from sunlight exposure
  - Items purchased from unauthorized resellers
  - Cosmetic damage (scratches, dents) that don't affect function

Q: How do I make a warranty claim?
A: Email support@nimbus.com with your order number, a description of the defect, and photos if possible. Our team will respond within 2 business days with next steps. Do not ship an item back without instruction — we'll tell you if a return is necessary.

==============================================
GIFT CARDS
==============================================

Q: Do Nimbus gift cards expire?
A: No. Nimbus gift cards never expire and have no fees.

Q: Can I use multiple gift cards on one order?
A: Yes, you can apply up to 3 gift cards per order at checkout.

Q: What if I lose my gift card?
A: Digital gift cards are tied to the email address they were sent to. Log in at nimbus.com and check your account balance under Settings → Gift Cards. Physical gift card balances can be checked at nimbus.com/giftcard — treat physical gift cards like cash; lost cards cannot be replaced without a receipt.

Q: Can I exchange a gift card for cash?
A: Gift cards cannot be exchanged for cash, except where required by law.

==============================================
PRODUCT QUESTIONS
==============================================

Q: Are Nimbus products ethically sourced?
A: Nimbus is committed to responsible sourcing. We audit our manufacturing partners annually against our Supplier Code of Conduct, which covers fair wages, safe working conditions, and environmental standards. Our sustainability report is published annually at nimbus.com/sustainability.

Q: Are Nimbus products safe for children and pets?
A: Many Nimbus products are designed for general household use and are not specifically tested or certified for child or pet safety. Items such as candles, essential oils, and certain cleaning products should be kept out of reach of children and pets. Check individual product pages for safety certifications.

Q: Do you sell replacement parts?
A: Replacement parts (e.g., wick holders for candles, reed sticks for diffusers) are available on nimbus.com on individual product pages. For other replacement needs, contact support@nimbus.com.

==============================================
PROMOTIONS & DISCOUNTS
==============================================

Q: Can I use more than one promo code at a time?
A: Only one promo code can be applied per order. Promo codes cannot be combined with each other, but can be combined with a Nimbus Premium member discount.

Q: My promo code isn't working — what do I do?
A: Common reasons promo codes fail:
  - The code has expired (check the email it came in)
  - The minimum order value hasn't been met
  - The items in your cart don't qualify (some codes exclude sale items or specific categories)
  - The code has already been used (most codes are single-use)
  Contact support with the code and we'll look into it.
